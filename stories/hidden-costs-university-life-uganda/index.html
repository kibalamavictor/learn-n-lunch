THE PRICE OF STUDYING: THE HIDDEN COSTS OF UNIVERSITY LIFE IN UGANDA

Higher education is often seen as a pathway to opportunity. For many students, entering university represents hope, ambition, and the possibility of building a better future.

However, the cost of achieving that future goes beyond tuition fees.

Across Uganda, students navigate multiple financial pressures that shape their university experience. Accommodation, transport, academic materials, and daily living expenses all influence whether students can fully participate in campus life.

These hidden costs are often overlooked in conversations about access to education.

Education Access Is More Than Enrollment

A student being admitted to university does not always mean they have equal access to success.

True access means having the conditions needed to learn, participate, and complete education successfully.

When students struggle to meet basic needs, their academic journey becomes more difficult. The challenge is not only getting into university, but being able to thrive while there.

The Need for Student-Centred Solutions

Building stronger universities requires understanding the realities students face beyond the classroom.

Institutions, policymakers, and partners must look at student experiences holistically and develop systems that respond to real challenges.

Supporting students means investing in the conditions that make learning possible.

Learn And Lunch: Supporting Student Success

At Learn And Lunch, we believe that student success is shaped by more than academic opportunities. It depends on whether students have the support systems needed to reach their potential.

Through research, advocacy, and innovative solutions, we are working to address challenges affecting university students in Uganda.

Creating better universities requires collective action.
