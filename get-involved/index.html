Frequently Asked Questions

Who can join the student coalition?

Any university student in Uganda who wants to advocate for campus welfare and food security can join. Coalition members mobilize peers, share evidence, and strengthen student-led dialogue on hunger.

How do organizations partner with Learn And Lunch?

Partners collaborate on research, nutrition programming, institutional dialogue, or event support. Email info@learnandlunch.org with your organization name and proposed collaboration.

Can I volunteer if I am not a student?

Yes. Supporters can volunteer at events, help amplify stories online, and contribute skills in communications, research, or logistics. Contact us to discuss how you can help.
