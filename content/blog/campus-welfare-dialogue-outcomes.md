## Why we convened

In August 2025, Learn N' Lunch hosted a **Campus Welfare Dialogue** bringing together student guild representatives, faculty welfare contacts, and program volunteers from four universities. The goal was not another abstract discussion about "student challenges" — it was a working session to agree on **specific, testable improvements** to meal access and welfare communication.

## Participants

- 22 student leaders (guild, faculty reps, ambassadors)
- 8 faculty / welfare office contacts
- 11 Learn N' Lunch staff and volunteers
- 2 community nutrition advisors

## Agreed outcomes

### 1. Publish a shared "where/when" schedule

Students repeatedly said confusion about distribution timing was the biggest barrier — not lack of interest. Campuses will pilot a standardized weekly schedule posted in three places: guild notice boards, a pinned WhatsApp message, and the Learn N' Lunch Stories page.

### 2. Create a student feedback channel

Each campus will maintain a simple Google Form linked from event signage. Responses reviewed weekly by ambassadors and shared monthly in a public summary (no personal identifiers published).

### 3. Improve volunteer handoffs

Campuses agreed to document volunteer roles using a one-page playbook so new volunteers can join mid-semester without losing quality.

![Discussion notes and action items from the dialogue](/new/frame-card-3.jpg)

## Student priorities (ranked)

From a live polling exercise at the dialogue:

1. **Predictable meal windows** aligned to lecture breaks
2. **Exam-period support** when skipping meals is most common
3. **Dignity-centered service** — no public labeling of students who receive meals
4. **Transparent reporting** on how many meals are served and where funding goes

> "We do not need perfection. We need consistency students can plan around."  
> — Student guild representative, Makerere

## Implementation timeline
