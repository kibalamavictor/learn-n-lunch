  heading: THE CAMPUS FOOD SECURITY CHALLENGE
  body: >-
    The cost of university life extends beyond tuition. Accommodation,
    transport, study materials and other expenses can leave students with
    limited resources for food. When money runs short or expected support is
    delayed, students may skip meals, reduce food quality or struggle to access
    adequate nutrition. These experiences are often managed quietly. Learn And
    Lunch works to understand these realities and support responses that protect
    student dignity while addressing the wider conditions that shape food
    access.
