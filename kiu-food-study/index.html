Research questionnaire

Food insecurity among students at KIU

Prevalence, determinants and consequences of food insecurity among undergraduate students at Kampala International University.

  • Enrolled KIU undergraduates aged 18+
  • About 15–20 minutes
  • Anonymous and confidential

This questionnaire is designed to assess the prevalence, socio-economic determinants and consequences of food insecurity among undergraduate students at Kampala International University. It is divided into sections A to J and is self-administered online.

Please answer all questions as accurately as possible. All information provided will be treated with strict confidentiality and used for academic research purposes only.
