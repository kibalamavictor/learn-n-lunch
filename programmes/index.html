OUR WORK

We combine research, practical implementation, student leadership and institutional development. Our work helps universities understand campus food insecurity, test solutions and strengthen the systems needed to sustain progress.

WHY THIS WORK MATTERS

Food security is part of the student experience and the wider conditions that shape educational participation.

Students need reliable access to adequate, nutritious and affordable food to participate fully in university life. Yet campus food insecurity is not always recognised in institutional planning or student support. Learn And Lunch works to close this gap through evidence, practical action and collaboration with universities and partners.

OUR APPROACH

From understanding campus food insecurity to implementing solutions and strengthening institutional responses.

We believe progress requires connected action. Research helps identify challenges. Student leadership ensures that students shape responses. Practical programmes test what works. Partnerships help universities and other stakeholders strengthen and sustain effective approaches.
