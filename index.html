Building Food-Secure Universities. Advancing Student Success.

Food security is essential to student wellbeing and educational success. Learn And Lunch works with students, universities and partners to understand campus food insecurity, implement practical solutions and strengthen the systems that make food-secure higher education possible.

HOW WE WORK

Research and Evidence

We generate evidence about student food insecurity, food access, affordability and campus food environments to inform practical responses and institutional decisions.

Student Leadership

We work through student associations, guilds and campus networks so students can shape solutions and strengthen food security action within their institutions.

Food Access and Innovation

We design, implement and test practical approaches to improve student access to adequate, nutritious and affordable food, including meal access, nutrition education and campus food initiatives.

Institutional Development

We work with universities and partners to develop the structures, policies, programmes and capacity needed to make food security part of higher education.

Partnership and Collective Action

We bring together universities, students, researchers, food-system actors and development partners to build shared responses to campus food insecurity.
